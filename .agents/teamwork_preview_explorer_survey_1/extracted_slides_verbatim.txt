⚡ PROBLEM & SOLUTION
PROPOSED SOLUTION: ASHA EMERGENCY RESUSCITATION GRID
Solving India's 1-in-5 Golden Hour Survival Crisis • Eliminating the Fatal 2.4 Gate Rejection Cycle
🚨 THE MASSIVE CRISIS: ONLY 1 IN 5 VICTIMS REACH CARE IN THE GOLDEN HOUR
▪ The National Tragedy: Over 150,000 Indians die each year inside ambulances—not from lack of medical cures, but because ambulances wander through an average of 2.4 hospital gate rejections.
▪ Read-Only State Directories: Current portals answer 'what existed hours ago', not 'is a bed held for me?' In acute shock, reading is not reserving.
