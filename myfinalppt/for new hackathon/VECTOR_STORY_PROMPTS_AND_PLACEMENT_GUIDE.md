## 🛠️ How to Insert Generated Images into PowerPoint

### Method A: Manual Insertion (Recommended for Quick Visual Polish)
1. Open `d:\HACKATHON\myfinalppt\for new hackathon\WhyCode4U_ASHA_Healthcare_For_Everyone.pptx` in Microsoft PowerPoint or WPS Office.
2. Navigate to the desired slide (e.g. Slide 2, 3, 5, or 8).
3. Select the phone inner screen placeholder (e.g., `Rounded Rectangle 32` or the slot marked `[ 📸 CLICK & PASTE SCREENSHOT / VECTOR ART HERE ]`).
4. Click **Insert $\rightarrow$ Pictures $\rightarrow$ This Device** (or drag and drop your generated image directly over the frame).
5. Right-click the inserted image $\rightarrow$ **Send to Back** (or **Send Backward**) so the subtle phone bezel borders frame your screenshot cleanly.

### Method B: Automated Python Script Replacement
If you save your generated images as:
- `slide2_story.png`
- `slide3_inclusivity.png`
- `slide5_firstaid.png`
- `slide8_admindesk.png`

Inside `d:\HACKATHON\myfinalppt\for new hackathon\images\`, you can run a simple 5-line `python-pptx` script to replace the placeholders programmatically:

```python
import pptx

from pptx.util import Inches

# Replace on Slide 2 (index 1)
```

## 🏆 Presentation Delivery Tip for Hackathon Judges

When presenting to judges:
1. **Open with Riya and Dadi (Slide 1 & 2):** Connect immediately with human empathy. Mention that in India, only 1 in 5 emergency victims reach care in the Golden Hour because families are forced to make blind phone calls to hospitals with static, unverified portals.
2. **Emphasize Inclusion (Slide 3):** Point out the Bengali vernacular speech triage and zero-install PWA—it works on a $50 Android phone over a patchy 2G/3G network with zero setup.
3. **Stand on Architectural Moats (Slide 4):** Clarify that ASHA is NOT another Google Maps clone or static dashboard. Our 6 architectural moats—specifically atomic 15-minute GPS soft locks and staffed bed calculations—prevent ambulances from racing to empty beds.
4. **Demonstrate Verifiable Reality (Slide 8):** Conclude by giving the judges the live production URLs (`ashaien.vercel.app` and `ashaien-admin.vercel.app`) and invite them to test the sub-second triage right on their phones!
