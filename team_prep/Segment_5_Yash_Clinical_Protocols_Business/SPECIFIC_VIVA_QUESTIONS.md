### Q1. "Why is Situational First-Aid considered ASHA's biggest plus point?"
**Winning Answer:** Because securing a bed is completely useless if the patient dies during the 30–45 minutes of transit. Over 50% of cardiac and trauma deaths occur before reaching the hospital due to bystander panic or fatal mistakes. Our situational sheet tells families *exactly what to do right now*—elevating a cardiac patient to Semi-Fowler 45° with bent knees, placing stroke patients in recovery position, and warning them never to give water to unconscious victims.

### Q2. "What is a 'Ghost Bed' and how does your mathematical formula eliminate it?"
**Winning Answer:** A ghost bed is a physical bed frame listed on a dashboard that cannot accept patients because no nurse is on duty to staff it. ASHA calculates usable beds strictly as $\text{Staffed Beds} = \min(\text{Physical Beds}, \text{On-Duty Nurses} \times \text{Ratio})$ based on Indian Society of Critical Care Medicine (ISCCM) and NHSRC standards (1:1 ICU, 1:2 HDU, 1:6 General Ward). Furthermore, our allocation algorithm sheds general ward beds first during staff reductions to protect ICU beds, and activates an emergency **1:2 ICU Surge Stretch Ratio** during severe staff shortages (1 critical nurse monitors 2 adjacent telemetry ICU beds), doubling ICU capacity and preventing premature gate rejections!

### Q3. "What is the revenue and monetization model for scaling ASHA nationwide?"
**Winning Answer:** We operate a hybrid B2G and B2B model. For state governments (National Health Mission / Smart City grants), ASHA is licensed as a G2C public emergency infrastructure grid. For private hospital networks, we charge a B2B SaaS subscription for pre-arrival triage integration, automated bed occupancy telemetry, and EHR interoperability via ABDM / HL7 FHIR.

### Q4. "Why will ASHA succeed when government portals like Swasthya Sathi or 108 struggle?"
**Winning Answer:** Government portals failed because they were static, manual desktop websites updated once a day by administrative clerks who didn't work nights. ASHA succeeds because of zero-signup instant mobile access, real-time 15-minute GPS soft-locks, staffed bed math, and hands-free Bengali speech translation.
