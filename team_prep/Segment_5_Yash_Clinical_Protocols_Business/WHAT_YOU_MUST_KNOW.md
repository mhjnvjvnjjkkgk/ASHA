## 🌟 The Big Picture: Why ASHA Exists
In India, **over 50,000 patients die every year** not because hospital beds don't exist, but because:
1. **Ambulances bounce across 2.4 hospitals** with dying patients before finding an open bed.
2. **Government dashboards show 'Ghost Beds'**—beds that exist as physical furniture but have zero nurses to staff them.
3. **The 30–45 minute transit period is a fatal void** where panicked families do nothing or commit fatal mistakes like pouring water down unconscious throats.

ASHA fixes this with a **zero-signup citizen web app** (`ashaien.vercel.app`) and a **zero-overhead hospital command desk** (`ashaien-admin.vercel.app`).

### 5. Staffed Bed vs. Ghost Bed Formula (ISCCM / NHSRC Standards)
- **The Formula:** $\text{Staffed Beds} = \min(\text{Physical Beds}, \text{Nurses} \times \text{Ratio})$.
- **Statutory Standard:** Indian Society of Critical Care Medicine (ISCCM) & National Health Systems Resource Centre (NHSRC).
- **Standard Ratios:** Intensive Care Unit (ICU) **1:1**, High Dependency Unit (HDU/PICU) **1:2**, General/Oxygen Ward **1:6**.
- **Normal Bed Shedding Priority:** When shift nurses drop, normal general ward beds are deducted first to protect critical ICU resuscitation beds.
- **Emergency 1:2 ICU Surge Stretch Ratio:** When staffing drops critically low ($\le 4$ nurses or unable to cover ICU at 1:1), the ICU ratio automatically stretches to **1:2** (1 nurse per 2 adjacent ICU beds), doubling ICU admission capacity and preventing fatal gate rejections!

### 6. Golden Hour Trauma Epidemiology (The Lancet Global Health & AIIMS)
- **1 in 5 Survival:** Only 1 in 5 (20%) emergency victims in India reach medical care within the Golden Hour.
- **2.4 Rejection Cycle:** Ambulances bounce through an average of 2.4 hospital gate rejections before admission.
- **Transit Void Mortality:** Over 50% of trauma and cardiac deaths occur en route inside the vehicle. ASHA bridges this transit void with instant First-Aid positioning.
